Table talk

    How it works

    Hit to flip a card, Stay to bank your round.

    Flip a number you already hold and you bust — that round scores nothing, unless you hold a Second Chance, which is discarded to save you.

    Collect 7 unique numbers and the round ends immediately with a +15 bonus.

    +N adds to your round. x2 doubles your number cards only.

    Freeze forces a player to stay. Flip Three forces three flips. Second Chance survives one bust.

    First to 200 points wins.
