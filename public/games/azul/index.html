How to play

  1. Take every tile of one colour from a factory — the rest slide to the centre — or take one colour from the centre.
  2. The first player to take from the centre also takes the 1st-player marker onto their floor line, and starts the next round.
  3. Drop the tiles on one pattern line. A line holds a single colour, and never a colour already tiled in that wall row. Extras fall to the floor.
  4. When the factories and centre are empty, each full pattern line moves one tile to the wall and scores; leftovers are discarded.
  5. The game ends after the round in which someone completes a horizontal wall row.
