How to play
Move 1–3 of your own marbles that sit adjacent in a straight line. In-line — the column shifts along its own axis and may push. Broadside — the line steps sideways; it can never push. Sumito: you may push only when you strictly outnumber the opponent marbles directly ahead — 2v1, 3v1, 3v2. Never 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3. A push is blocked by any marble of your own colour behind the opponent group. Marbles shoved past the rim are out. First to push 6 enemy marbles off the board wins.
