How this solo challenge works

Numbers score their face value. A duplicate number ends the turn with no points. Seven different number cards end the turn and add 15 points. Freeze banks your hand, Flip Three draws three cards, and Second Chance cancels one duplicate. Plus cards add points; x2 doubles number points.
